Credits & Inspiration
=====================

Inspired by:
------------
Commercial minimalist digital LED wall clocks showing time, day-of-week indicator, and full calendar dates, alongside the practical need to repurpose older hardware like the Lenovo TB3-710F tablet.

Patterned on:
-------------
Classic 7-segment hardware LED displays, adapted for vector Canvas rendering in Jetpack Compose without font file scaling artifacts.

Previously used app for this purpose:
-------------------------------------
None.

Built-in libraries & tools:
---------------------------
- Jetpack Compose, AndroidX ViewModel, and Jetpack DataStore (Preferences)
- Multiplatform Markdown Renderer (`com.mikepenz:multiplatform-markdown-renderer-m3`) for About screen asset rendering
- Agentic engineering assistance from Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google)
